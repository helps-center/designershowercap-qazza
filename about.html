Our Stuff: Where usefulness meets style

We have different kinds of caps made for today's people:

1. Daily protection

These caps are for when you shower every day. They keep your hair dry and look good while doing it. They're great for all kinds of hair, from short to long and thick.

2. Hair treatments &amp; spa days

Our caps are good for deep conditioning, hair masks, and heating treatments. The outside keeps water out, and the inside keeps your hair from getting frizzy or breaking.

3. Traveling

Our caps are easy to pack because they fold up small and don't weigh much. They keep your hair safe when you're traveling. They fit in your bag and are strong enough to use over and over.

4. Fancy Style

We make our caps look good by adding cool designs, shiny bits, patterns and soft edges. When you put on one of our caps, you're showing people you care about yourself and have good taste.

Good Materials

The hair science

We know hair is sensitive and needs looking after. Our caps are made with that in mind:

Less rubbing: The inside keeps your hair from rubbing against itself, which helps prevent damage.

Dry hair: Waterproof stuff keeps your hair dry, which stops it from getting frizzy.

Heat: Some caps keep heat in, which helps hair treatments work better.

Healthy hair: Our caps keep your hair safe from getting damaged, so it stays shiny and strong.
